Preventing Accidental Border Violations

Fishermen often cross international maritime boundaries unintentionally due to strong ocean currents, night navigation, or lack of visual boundary markers. OceanMind acts as an intelligent digital guardian, calculating safe buffer distances and alerting captains before entering dangerous territorial waters.
